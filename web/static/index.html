Fear Studio Retention Checklist & Targets

              

              

                

                  The 0:15 Cold Open
                  
Target >70% retention at 30 seconds. Do not insert logos or channel intros. Jump straight into the eerie setup.

                

                

                  Pacing Beats (130-145 wpm)
                  
Horror requires breathing room. Slow down deliberately before reveal moments to build suffocating tension.

                

                

                  Thumbnail Pairing
                  
Avoid duplicating title words on the thumbnail image. 3 words max on thumb (e.g. "DON'T LOOK BACK").
